1. “A clinical operations team wants an agent that reads resumes, hospital questionnaires, and prior clinical testing results to draft sections of a Clinical Trials Protocol, then emails the draft to the study coordinator. A business analyst built it in a low-code tool over a weekend. Outline how you would risk tier it for me.”
He can better explain how he would risk tier the agent based on what it reads, what it drafts, who receives the draft, and the risks associated with the Clinical Trials Protocol.
2. “Walk me through the risk tiering model you'd apply; what would you score? What would you use to score? How do you define each tier?” “What are a few exceptions that can move the agent up or down a tier” “Which dimension would you not move on?”
He can better explain the risk tiering model, what he would score, what he would use to score, how he would define each tier, and which exceptions could move an agent up or down a tier.
3. “An employee has an idea for an internal AI agent today, and eighteen months from now that agent is retired. Walk me through everything that happens in between: every step, every artifact, every control, wherever your knowledge goes deepest.”
He can better explain every step, every artifact, and every control across the AI agent lifecycle from an employee idea through development, deployment, operation, and retirement.
4. “An underlying model gets upgraded by the hosting platform overnight. Which of your agents do you re-review, and how do you know?”
He can better explain which agents he would re-review after an underlying model gets upgraded and how he would know which agents are affected.
5. “You have two mandates that pull against each other: accelerate agent adoption across every business unit and prevent uncontrolled proliferation. You manage no one, you have no headcount and no tooling budget, a VP has already told one business unit their unregistered agent can stay, and going to leadership for a mandate is off the table. How would you run both mandates at once”
He can better explain how he would accelerate agent adoption across every business unit while preventing uncontrolled proliferation, despite having no headcount, no tooling budget, and no mandate.
